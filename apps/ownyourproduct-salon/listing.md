## A salon's whole product, under your own name

Calendar and booking window, clients, services and staff, reports and expenses, a five-step
set-up with prices anchored to the salon's country, and Instagram answered with the salon's
own prices — in Spanish, Portuguese and English. This is a finished salon product, not a
starter: the same screens a salon already runs its day on.

## On the Chatfuel engine

Bookings, contacts and Instagram go through Chatfuel. Sign-in, the team's levels and the
salon's own records live in your Supabase project. There is no separate server to keep: the
product's few server routes mount inside the engine's proxy, behind its sign-in.

## Yours to change

The wizard sets up the engine; the build plan then copies the whole front into your
repository — screens, copy, styles — and wires it in. From then on it is your code: change
the brand, the texts, the flows, or point the plan at your own fork of the front.
